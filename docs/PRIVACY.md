# Sparky IPTV — Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Sparky IPTV is a player for an IPTV service you already subscribe to. It comes with no channels, films or series of its own. This policy covers the Google TV, Windows and Mac apps.

## What the app stores, and where
- **Your provider login** (server address, username and password). It is kept **only on your device**, encrypted with the device's own key store: Android Keystore, Windows Data Protection (DPAPI) or the macOS Keychain.
- **Your settings and history**: favourites, folders, resume points, recently watched, regions, audio and subtitle choices. They are kept only on your device.
- **A copy of your provider's channel, film and series lists**, so the app opens quickly. It is kept only on your device.
- **An activity log** for troubleshooting, kept on your device. Passwords and usernames are replaced with ••• in every line.

## What the app sends, and to whom
- **To your IPTV provider**: your login and requests for lists, guide data and streams. This is the provider you chose; their own privacy policy applies to what they do with it.
- **To GitHub** (Google TV app only): a check for a newer version of the app. No personal data is sent.
- **To your phone, on your home network, only when you scan a QR code on screen**: the setup, backup, restore and log pages. These pages run on your TV or computer, need the one-time key in the QR code, and stop after 10 minutes without use.

The app has **no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no accounts** of its own. Nothing is sent to the developer.

## Backups
A backup file is made only when you ask for one, and is saved to your phone. If it contains your login, the login is encrypted with a passphrase you choose.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. On Windows and Mac you can also delete the "Sparky IPTV" folder in your local application data (Windows: `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sparky IPTV`; Mac: `~/Library/Application Support/Sparky IPTV`).

## Children
The app isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
